Our approach

Everything starts with research into your product and audience. It shapes what we say, the script and the delivery. We work with brands, experts and agencies.

  1. 01

    We study your product and audience

    What matters to your customers, what holds them back and what drives their choice.

  2. 02

    We shape the message and the script

    What to say and how to say it, so the content does a job for the business instead of just filling the feed.

  3. 03

    We produce the content

    We pick the AI tools for the task: the image quality, animation and timeline you need.

  4. 04

    We test and improve

    We read the performance data and build the next versions on it.

Services

Four services. Each comes as production to your brief, or as full marketing strategy plus production.

01

Branded AI series

A series of short videos with one concept, recurring characters and a recognisable style.
  • Timeline2–4 weeks
  • PriceAfter a brief
  • Formats2 to choose from

Instead of one-off videos, you get a story you can keep telling. Adapt it for different platforms and use it at every stage, from first contact with the brand to the decision to buy.

Who it's for

  • Brands building awareness and loyalty
  • Products with a complex idea that a story explains better than text
  • Anyone who wants to stand out from look-alike content
Production

You already have the concept, the style and the storylines. We take them as our base and produce the episodes.

What's included

  1. Briefing and goal-setting
  2. Episode scripts based on your concept
  3. Storyboards
  4. Generation, AI animation and editing
  5. Sound design
Strategy + Production

We study the product and audience and define the series' role in your brand communication. Then we build the series from the ground up, from the core message to the final videos.

What's included

  1. Briefing and goal-setting
  2. Product and audience research
  3. Defining the series' role in brand communication
  4. Core message and storylines, tied to business goals
  5. A unique style for the characters and the world
  6. Story arc (one or several episodes)
  7. Episode scripts
  8. Storyboards
  9. Generation, AI animation and editing
  10. Sound design

Strategy work can also include a message map, a content funnel and a story rubric. We set the scope for each project.

Timeline — 2–4 weeks

From brief to the first finished episodes. It depends on the concept, the number of episodes and how fast approvals go.

Price — quoted per project

Depends on the format, the number and length of episodes and the visual style. You get an exact quote in a proposal after the brief.
